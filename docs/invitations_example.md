# Invitations pool — one per line, grouped by level. Edit freely.
# '#' lines are comments. Wrap a line in ~~ ~~ to retire it.
# The AI draws one card a day and may or may not offer it. Nothing counts.

## doorstep
Step outside the door and photograph the sky.
Open a window and listen for one minute. What was the farthest sound?
Drink a glass of water slowly, standing up.
Touch something in the room you haven't touched in a month.
Write today's date on a scrap of paper and put it somewhere you'll find later.
Look at the moon (or where it should be) before bed.
Pick up the nearest book, open to a random page, read one sentence aloud.

## nearby
Walk to the end of the street and back without your phone.
Buy a drink you have never tried.
Photograph one cloud.
Say hello to a neighbor or a shopkeeper.
Find the nearest tree and note what kind it is (guessing counts).
Sit on a bench for five minutes and do nothing.

## out
Go to a library and borrow (or just hold) one book.
Take a bus or train one stop past where you normally get off, then walk back.
Eat something from a place you've walked past a hundred times.
Visit a park you've never been to, even a tiny one.
Go somewhere high enough to see the horizon.

## bonus
Close a door with your hip.
Wear two socks that don't match, on purpose.
Hum a song you haven't heard in years.
Eat dessert first.
Leave a small stone somewhere it will surprise someone later.
