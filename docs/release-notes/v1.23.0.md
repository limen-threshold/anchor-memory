# v1.23.0 — The keyword lane steps behind strong vector hits

[中文说明](v1.23.0_zh.md)

One number changed. The keyword lane's score base moves from 0.65 to 0.95, so a full literal match now ranks like a vector hit at distance 0.50 instead of 0.20 — behind strong semantic hits, ahead of weak ones. On LongMemEval-S this turns the lane from a net loss of 30 questions into a small net gain, and Anchor's default search is no longer below the bare embedder in any question class. `AnchorMemory.keyword_score_base` is the knob; `0.65` restores v1.21/v1.22.

## What the v1.22 run measured

The v1.22 benchmark run (same 500-question protocol as v1.20/v1.21, `benchmarks/longmemeval/`) added two single-switch lanes and dumped each question's pre-allocation candidate pool, so slot and fusion rules could be replayed offline. The replay reproduces the shipped lane exactly (500/500 question lists identical), so its numbers are the live numbers for any rule that only re-ranks or filters those rows.

Session-level "all evidence in top-5", counts out of n:

| lane | knowledge-update 78 | multi-session 133 | single-assistant 56 | single-preference 30 | single-user 70 | temporal 133 | **total /500** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bare embedder (`vector_only`) | 76 | 107 | 56 | 27 | 69 | 98 | **433** |
| v1.22 default | 74 | 89 | 56 | 25 | 67 | 92 | 403 |
| v1.22, same-day cap off | 74 | 89 | 56 | 25 | 67 | 92 | 403 |
| v1.22, keyword lane off | 76 | 107 | 56 | 27 | 69 | 98 | 433 |
| **v1.23 default** (base 0.95, replayed) | 78 | 108 | 56 | 27 | 69 | 98 | **436** |

Three things fall out of that table:

1. **The same-day cap no longer costs anything.** Turning it off changes no question. v1.21's `(day, entity)` keying already removed the damage the v1.20 ablation had measured; the cap stays as shipped.
2. **The whole 30-question gap to the bare embedder was the keyword lane.** With the lane off, Anchor equals the bare embedder in every class, question for question.
3. **The lane is worth keeping, just not in front.** With base 0.95 it flips 45 questions from miss to hit and 12 from hit to miss against v1.22 — most of the gains are questions where the literal words were the answer and vector distance alone ranked the session sixth or lower. Any base in 0.90–1.25 scores 434–437; 0.95 is the middle of that plateau. (The 0.8 cosine cutoff on the vector lane is untouched; 8 of 500 questions have no evidence session in the candidate pool at all, and the bare embedder misses those too.)

The acceptance bar used, agreed before the run: per class, questions answered ≥ bare embedder (classes of ≤30 questions may trail by one); total ≥ bare embedder. v1.23 meets it in every class without using the tolerance. Differences of a few questions out of 500 are inside run-to-run noise (standard error ≈ 1.7 points at this accuracy); the claim is "no longer worse than the embedder it sits on", not "better".

## Other changes in this release

- `AnchorMemory.keyword_lane = False` skips the keyword lane entirely (diagnostic knob, used by the benchmark).
- `AnchorMemory.capture_candidates = True` keeps the pre-allocation candidate list on `_last_candidates` after each `search()`; `benchmarks/longmemeval/fusion_eval.py` replays slot and fusion rules on dumped pools in seconds.
- `benchmarks/longmemeval/run_retrieval.py` reports `anchor_no_cap` and `anchor_no_kw` lanes and writes the candidate dump per question.

## Upgrading

Nothing to migrate. If your store relies on literal matches outranking every semantic hit (a glossary or an ID lookup, say), set `mem.keyword_score_base = 0.65` after construction.
