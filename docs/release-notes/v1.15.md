# v1.15 — Invitations ("want to do something small together?")

The oldest Anchor design note (2026-03-13, from the user interviews) had a *task system*: the AI asks the person **"要不要跟我一起做什么？"** — an invitation, not a command — from "go to the door and photograph the sky" up to "borrow a book from the library". Do it → a real experience. Don't → still a shared memory. The reward was never points: **the AI writes the moment down and names it**, and it becomes a story the two of you own. It was scoped for a phone app with a GPS trigger ("hasn't left the block in three days").

v1.15 ships that, minus the GPS. One card a day, from a pool that is **the AI's own** — cards the AI writes from what it remembers the person wanting (the bookstore she mentioned, the drink he never tried). Anchor's point is that the AI owns its memory; the cards are that memory turned outward. The AI decides whether to offer a card; the person decides whether to do it. Nothing counts, nothing streaks, nothing nags.

*(v1.15.1: the pool was briefly shipped with a starter list as the default. That was wrong — a list someone else wrote is not the AI's. Starters in `docs/` are now opt-in only; the default pool is empty, and `invitation_add` is how it fills.)*

## What's in it

- **`anchor_invite.py`** — stdlib only, zero-LLM.
  - Pool: `<pinned_dir>/invitations.md`, one invitation per line under `## level` headings (`门口 / 附近 / 出门 / 彩蛋` or `doorstep / nearby / out / bonus`). `#` comments; `~~struck~~` lines are retired. **Empty by default.** The AI fills it with `add_card()` / the `invitation_add` tool, from its own memories; an optional `# from: …` note keeps each card traceable. Starter pools in `docs/invitations_example*.md` exist to show the format (`init_pool()` copies one in) and are never applied automatically.
  - `render_empty_block()` — when the pool is empty: an offer to write cards from memory. The proxy shows it at most once a week; an empty pool is a valid choice.
  - `today(pinned_dir, state_dir)` — same card all day, a new draw tomorrow, recently drawn cards avoided, bonus-level cards drawn about 1 in 4.
  - `mark_done(state_dir, name, note)` / `mark_skipped(state_dir)`.
  - `render_block(card, lang)` — the instruction block. Written as an offer: *yours to offer or not; their "no" is a complete answer.*
- **MCP tools**: `get_invitation`, `invitation_add(text, level, why)`, `invitation_done(name, note)` — stores `"<name>: <card> — <note>"` as a `together` / `long` memory with the card as context, and appends a `together · <name>` line to `recent_timeline.md` — and `invitation_skip`. `wakeup()` includes today's card while it is still open.
- **Proxy**: `build_invitation_block()` injects the card **once per day**, on the first turn after midnight, so it reads as a card handed over in the morning rather than a reminder on every message.

## What makes a good card

**Small, and yours.** A card can be about them (they said they like pink → "write your name once in a pink marker"), about the AI ("I'd like some sun from inside the phone — take me out for ten minutes"), or simply one small thing the AI would like them to do right now. Same act, the AI's own intent; no memory citation required. Doing it is always the person's choice. Minutes-sized is perfect. The one truly special thing (the trip, the restaurant across town) goes under the `bonus` / `彩蛋` level, which is drawn about one day in four so it stays a surprise rather than the daily default.

## What it deliberately is not

- Not a habit tracker. No streaks, no scores, no "you skipped 3 days".
- Not location-aware. The original GPS trigger is gone; the card is just there each day.
- Not a directive to the model. The block says *may*, and the AI's choice not to extend the card is respected by the mechanism (an unmarked card simply expires).

## Compatibility

Additive. No pool file → every path is silent (`get_invitation` returns null with a hint, `wakeup` and the proxy add nothing). serverInfo version → 1.15.
